# Product Context

## Problem Statement
In today's world of endless food varieties and flavors, people often struggle to remember their experiences with specific products. Whether it's a particular chocolate brand, a specific flavor of snack, or a unique food item, consumers frequently forget which variants they enjoyed most. Tastier solves this by providing a simple way to record and recall personal food experiences, helping users make confident purchasing decisions based on their previous tastings.

## User Personas
### Primary Users
- Regular Food Shoppers: People who try different food products and want to remember their preferences
- Food Enthusiasts: Users who frequently try new flavors and variants of products
- Brand-Conscious Consumers: People who care about specific brands and their different product lines
- Memory-Aided Shoppers: Users who have trouble remembering their previous food experiences

### Secondary Users
- Family Shoppers: People buying food for family members with specific taste preferences
- Gift Buyers: Users who want to remember which treats others enjoyed

## User Journey
1. User finds an interesting food product or tries a new flavor
2. Opens app and quickly scans the product's barcode
3. Rates the product and optionally adds notes about the taste
4. Later, while shopping, scans the same product's barcode
5. Instantly recalls their previous rating and notes
6. Makes an informed decision about purchasing based on their recorded experience

## Key Features
- Fast barcode scanning for quick product lookup
- Simple rating system for food items
- Personal notes for taste descriptions
- Quick search by product name or brand
- Offline access to all ratings
- Dark/light theme for comfortable shopping

## User Experience Goals
- Quick and Easy: Scan and rate products in seconds
- Shopping-Friendly: Easy to use while in stores
- Memory Aid: Reliable recall of previous experiences
- Personal: Private tracking of individual preferences
- Offline-First: No internet needed for any function

## Market Context
While there are many shopping and product review apps, Tastier fills a specific niche for personal food preference tracking. Unlike general shopping apps or social review platforms, it focuses solely on helping individuals remember their own experiences with food products.

## Product Vision
To be the go-to personal food memory tool that helps people consistently enjoy their favorite foods by never forgetting which variants and flavors they preferred.

## Success Metrics
- Speed of Recording: Time to scan and rate a product
- Recall Efficiency: Time to find previous ratings
- Usage Frequency: Regular use during shopping
- Product Database: Number of personally rated items
- User Satisfaction: Ease of finding previous ratings

## Known Limitations
- Requires readable barcodes on products
- Limited to products with barcodes
- No sharing of ratings between users
- No cloud backup of personal ratings
- No product information database

## Future Considerations
- Optional categories for different types of food
- Favorites list for quick access
- Simple export of personal ratings
- Basic statistics about preferences
- Quick filters for common searches
- Notes templates for common attributes
