# Project Brief

## Overview
Tastier is a personal food tracking application that helps users remember their experiences with specific food products. The app solves a common problem: forgetting how much you liked a particular food item, such as a specific chocolate brand or flavor. Users can scan product barcodes to save their ratings and notes, making it easy to recall their preferences during future shopping trips.

## Core Requirements
- Quick barcode scanning for instant product identification in stores
- Simple rating system for food items
- Personal notes for taste descriptions and preferences
- Easy lookup of previously rated items via barcode scan or search
- Local database to store personal food ratings and preferences
- Offline functionality for use while shopping

## Project Scope
### In Scope
- Personal food product rating system
- Barcode scanning for quick product identification
- Local storage of ratings and preferences
- Basic product information (name, brand, type)
- Simple search and filter by product attributes
- Dark/light theme support for shopping comfort

### Out of Scope
- Online product database or catalog
- Social features or sharing capabilities
- Nutritional information tracking
- Price tracking or comparison
- Shopping list management
- Multiple user profiles

## Success Criteria
- Users can quickly scan and rate food products
- Previously rated products can be easily found via barcode scan
- Ratings and notes are stored reliably for future reference
- App works offline in stores
- Interface is simple enough to use while shopping

- Expo framework limitations

### Dependencies

- SQLite for local storage

## Risk Assessment
### Technical Risks
- Barcode scanner accuracy in various lighting conditions
- Local storage capacity for heavy users
- Database performance with large number of items

### Mitigation Strategies
- Optimize barcode scanning for various conditions
- Efficient data storage implementation
- Regular testing with large datasets
